INVESTMENT DILIGENCE MEMO — HEALTHBRIDGE DIAGNOSTICS
Series A Due Diligence | Prepared for: Impact Ventures Fund III
Date: October 2023

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

HealthBridge Diagnostics, based in Medellín, Colombia, develops portable AI-powered diagnostic devices for rural and underserved clinics across Latin America. Founded in 2021 by Dr. María Elena Rodríguez and Dr. Carlos Gutierrez, the company has deployed 340 devices across Colombia, Peru, and Ecuador, enabling lab-quality testing for 15+ conditions without the need for specialized laboratory equipment or trained pathologists.

The company is raising a $10M Series A to expand to Mexico, Brazil, and Central America, and to add 8 new diagnostic capabilities to its platform.

TECHNOLOGY & PRODUCT

The HealthBridge device is a tablet-sized diagnostic unit that uses computer vision and machine learning to analyze blood samples, urine samples, and tissue slides. Key capabilities:

- Complete blood count (CBC) with 96% accuracy vs. lab standards
- Malaria rapid detection (98.5% sensitivity)
- Diabetes screening (HbA1c)
- Tuberculosis screening
- Cervical cancer pre-screening
- 10+ additional tests in development

The AI models were trained on datasets from Hospital Pablo Tobón Uribe in Medellín and validated with the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO).

Cost per test: $2-8 (vs. $15-50 for equivalent lab tests)
Device cost: $4,500 (vs. $50,000+ for traditional lab equipment)

FOUNDING TEAM

RISK ANALYSIS

1. Regulatory risk: Each new country requires separate medical device approval (6-18 month process)
2. Accuracy risk: AI model performance in diverse populations must be continuously validated
3. Competition risk: Large diagnostic companies (Roche, Abbott) could develop competing products
4. Reimbursement risk: Government health insurance coverage for AI diagnostics uncertain
5. Key person risk: Dr. Rodríguez is the primary relationship holder with PAHO and government health ministries
6. Supply chain: Specialized components sourced from Shenzhen, China — geopolitical risk

ESG PROFILE

Environmental: Low energy consumption devices (solar-chargeable), minimal waste per test, recycling program for consumables
Social: 60% female leadership team, local manufacturing partnership in Medellín, training program for rural health workers (400+ trained)
Governance: Board includes independent directors, quarterly impact reporting, data privacy framework compliant with Colombian and EU standards
Controversies: None

RECOMMENDATION

Strong investment candidate. HealthBridge addresses a genuine access gap in healthcare diagnostics with a technically differentiated product and experienced team. Key risks are regulatory timeline and competitive moat durability. Recommend proceeding to term sheet with key conditions around regulatory milestones and clinical validation benchmarks.
